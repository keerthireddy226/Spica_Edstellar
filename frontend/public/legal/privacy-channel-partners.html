Privacy Notice: Channel Partners

Version 2026-11-18 · Draft for legal review

In short: as a Channel Partner, you give us your name, email and business details so you can use the partner portal. We use them to run the partnership: your account, your demo licences, your orders, invoices and commission. You also see a little data about the people at the organisations you bring (names, emails, licence status and whether tests are done), and you must keep it private and use it only to support those organisations.

1. Who this notice is for

This notice is for people and companies registered as a Channel Partner of Spica: partners who bring universities, colleges and companies to Spica, give them demo licences and place orders for them. It explains what Edstellar, which runs Spica, does with your own data, and what you may do with the data you see about others.

2. Who is responsible

For your own data (as a partner), Edstellar is the controller (in India, the "data fiduciary"). Privacy contact and Grievance Officer: the Spica Super Admin, at [privacy contact email].

3. What we collect about you

  • Account details: your full name, email address, password (stored only in scrambled, hashed form), language, optional two-step sign-in settings and your partner code.
  • Business details: the commission rate, demo licence allocation and demo window agreed with you, notes Spica staff keep about the partnership, and the bank or payment details you give us to receive commission.
  • Activity: the organisations you add, the demo licences you give, the orders you place, the invoices and payments on them, the commission due and paid, and a record of each action (who did it and when).
  • Technical data: sign-in times, IP address and browser, to keep your account secure.

4. Why we use it

UseLegal basis

Demo licences, orders, proforma and tax invoices, and paying your commissionOur agreement with you; our legal duties (tax and accounting records)

We do not sell your data and do not use it for advertising.

5. Who sees your data

  • Spica staff who manage the partner programme, Finance staff for invoices and commission, and Super Admins.
  • The admins of the organisations you bring see that their organisation was brought by you, and your name on their orders.
  • Service providers that work for us under contract: website delivery (Vercel), hosting and storage (Render and Supabase, in Singapore), email (Brevo) and payments (Stripe and Razorpay).
  • Our accountants and auditors, and authorities when the law requires it.

6. The data you see about others, and your duties

For each organisation you bring, the portal shows you: the organisation's admin (name, email, whether they have signed in), and for each learner their name, email address, licence type (demo or paid) and end date, and whether they started, finished the mock test and finished a real test (yes or no). You never see scores, levels, answers, recordings, reports or proctoring data.

You may use this information only to help that organisation use Spica (for example, to remind them that licences are unused). You must not copy it into other systems, sell it, use it for your own marketing, or share it with anyone else. You must keep it secure and tell Spica at once if you think it has been lost or seen by someone who should not see it. These duties are also in the Channel Partner Agreement.

7. Where your data is stored

On secure cloud servers in Singapore (Render and Supabase). Requests pass through Vercel's global network. Where data leaves India, we use contracts that protect it.

8. How long we keep it

  • Your account and partnership records: while you are a partner, and for 12 months after the partnership ends.
  • Orders, invoices and commission records: 8 years, as Indian tax law requires.
  • The record of actions (audit log): 12 months.

9. Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete your data, to object to a use based on our legitimate interest, and to get a copy of your data. Some data (invoices, commission) must be kept for the legal periods above. You can also complain to the Data Protection Board of India or to your local data protection authority.

10. Security

Encrypted connections, hashed passwords, optional two-step sign-in, access limited by role, and an audit log of who did what. Deactivated partners are signed out and cannot sign in.

11. Changes

If we change this notice, we will tell you in the partner portal or by email before the change applies.

12. Contact

The Spica Super Admin, Grievance Officer: [privacy contact email].
