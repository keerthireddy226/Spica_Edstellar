Spica by Edstellar

Privacy Notice: Partners

Version 2026-11-19 · Resellers, Channel Partners, Affiliate Partners and Referral Partners · Draft for legal review

In short: as a Spica partner, you give us your name, email and business details so you can use the partner portal. We use them to run the partnership: your account, your signed agreement, and, depending on your partner type, your organisations, demo licences, orders, invoices, referrals and earnings. You see only the data your partner type needs, never anyone's scores or reports, and you must keep what you see private.

1. Who this notice is for

This notice is for people and companies registered as a Spica partner:

  • Resellers buy Spica licences at a discount and resell them to their own customers.
  • Channel Partners bring universities, colleges and companies to Spica, give them demo licences and place orders for them.
  • Affiliate Partners promote Spica with their own link and code and earn a commission on purchases made through them.
  • Referral Partners introduce organisations to Spica and earn a fee when one becomes a customer.

It explains what Edstellar, which runs Spica, does with your own data, and what you may do with the data you see about others.

2. Who is responsible

For your own data (as a partner), Edstellar is the controller (in India, the "data fiduciary"). Privacy contact and Grievance Officer: the Spica Super Admin, at [privacy contact email].

3. What we collect about you

  • Account details: your full name, email address, password (stored only in scrambled, hashed form), language, optional two-step sign-in settings, your partner type and partner code.
  • Partnership details: the start and end dates of each 3-month partnership period, renewals (and any reason recorded for renewing below the organisation minimum), termination and its reason, the minimums and rates agreed with you (commission, reseller discount or referral fee), notes Spica staff keep about the partnership, and the bank or payment details you give us to be paid.
  • Your signed agreement: for each period, the name you typed to sign, your email, the date and time, your IP address and browser, and the version of the agreement you signed.
  • Activity: the organisations you add, the demo licences you give, the orders you place, the referrals you submit, the invoices and payments, the commission or fees due and paid, and a record of each action (who did it and when).
  • Technical data: sign-in times, IP address and browser, to keep your account secure.

5. Who sees your data

  • Spica staff who manage the partner programme, Finance staff for invoices and payments, and Super Admins.
  • Resellers and Channel Partners: the admins of the organisations you bring see that their organisation came through you. A Channel Partner's name appears on the orders it places; a Reseller's orders with Spica are not shown to its customers.
  • Affiliate and Referral Partners: we do not tell the people or organisations you refer anything about you beyond what you tell them yourself.
  • Service providers that work for us under contract: website delivery (Vercel), hosting and storage (Render and Supabase, in Singapore), email (Brevo) and payments (Stripe and Razorpay).
  • Our accountants and auditors, and authorities when the law requires it.

6. The data you see about others, and your duties

Partner typeWhat the portal shows you
Reseller and Channel PartnerFor each organisation you bring: its admin (name, email, whether they have signed in), and for each learner their name, email address, licence type (demo or paid) and end date, and whether they started, finished the mock test and finished a real test (yes or no).

Referral PartnerThe details you submitted about each organisation and its contact, and the status Spica gives your referral (submitted, contacted, won, lost or already known). Not the organisation's account or its people.

No partner ever sees scores, levels, answers, recordings, reports, certificates or proctoring data.

You may use this information only for the partnership (for example, to remind an organisation that its licences are unused). You must not copy it into other systems, sell it, use it for your own marketing, or share it with anyone else. You must keep it secure and tell Spica at once if you think it has been lost or seen by someone who should not see it.

When you refer someone (Referral Partners, and Resellers and Channel Partners adding an organisation): you must have the contact person's agreement to share their name, email and phone with Edstellar and for Edstellar to contact them about Spica. The portal asks you to confirm this each time. Edstellar then becomes responsible for that contact's data and gives them its own privacy information when it contacts them.

These duties are also in your partnership agreement.

7. When the partnership ends

When your partnership period ends without renewal, or Spica ends it early, your portal becomes read-only: you can still see your history, invoices and earnings, but not add organisations, give licences, order or refer. Your data is kept as described in section 9.

8. Where your data is stored

On secure cloud servers in Singapore (Render and Supabase). Requests pass through Vercel's global network. Where data leaves India, we use contracts that protect it.

9. How long we keep it

  • Your account and partnership records, including signed agreements and renewals: while you are a partner, and for 12 months after the partnership ends; signed agreements for as long as a claim under them is possible [lawyer to confirm the period].
  • Referrals that did not become customers: 12 months after the last update.
  • Orders, invoices, commission and fee payment records: 8 years, as Indian tax law requires.
  • The record of actions (audit log): 12 months.

10. Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete your data, to object to a use based on our legitimate interest, and to get a copy of your data. Some data (signed agreements, invoices, payments) must be kept for the periods above. You can also complain to the Data Protection Board of India or to your local data protection authority.

11. Security

Encrypted connections, hashed passwords, optional two-step sign-in, access limited by partner type and role, and an audit log of who did what. Deactivated partners are signed out and cannot sign in.

12. Changes

If we change this notice, we will tell you in the partner portal or by email before the change applies.

13. Contact

The Spica Super Admin, Grievance Officer: [privacy contact email].
