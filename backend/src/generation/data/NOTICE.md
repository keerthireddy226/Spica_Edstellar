# Word and grammar lists

`cefrWords.ts` and `cefrGrammar.ts` are built from the Open Language Profiles
datasets (https://github.com/openlanguageprofiles/olp-en-cefrj):

- CEFR-J Wordlist Version 1.5 and CEFR-J Grammar Profile (2018-03-15).
  Tono Laboratory, Tokyo University of Foreign Studies. Free for research and
  commercial use when cited: "The CEFR-J Wordlist Version 1.5. Compiled by Yukio
  Tono, Tokyo University of Foreign Studies."
- Octanove Vocabulary Profile C1/C2 version 1.0, Creative Commons
  Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 International (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/).
  The C1 and C2 words in `cefrWords.ts` come from it, so that file is shared under
  the same licence.

Each word keeps its lowest listed level. Used only to check that a question's
words suit its level.
